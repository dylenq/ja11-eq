`ja11-eq` output contract (stdout), fixed here so plan 02-04 can match it:

```text
backup: saved /Users/…/Library/Application Support/JA11 EQ/user1-original.json     (or: backup: kept <path>)
backup-time: 2026-10-10T15:04:05Z
original: slot: 3 (USER1)                 (backup subcommand only: the eight EQState.reportLines() of the backup)
original: pre-gain: +0.0 dB
…
sent: slot: 3 (USER1)                     (apply and restore: the eight report lines of what was sent)
sent: pre-gain: -6.0 dB
…
link: disconnected +0.42s                 (zero or more, as seen after the save)
link: connected +2.10s
read: slot: 3 (USER1)                     (the eight report lines of the read-back)
…
diff: filter 1 gain: sent -3.0 dB, read +0.0 dB      (zero or more)
final: slot: 3 (USER1)                    (restore only)
result: confirmed                         (or: result: mismatch)
```

For `slot <name>`: `sent: slot: 0 (Vocal)`, `read: slot: 0 (Vocal)`, then the state in that slot as
`state: ` lines or one `info: state not readable in this slot: <error>` line, then the `result:` line.
With `--raw`, `tx: ` and `rx: ` hex lines as `ja11-probe --raw` prints them. Errors go to stderr.
